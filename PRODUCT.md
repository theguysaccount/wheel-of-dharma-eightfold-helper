# Product: Eightfold Helper

**User.** People exploring or practising Buddhism who want a simple, honest way to see which parts of the Noble Eightfold Path to give attention to.

**Painful job.** The eightfold path is easy to list and hard to apply. Most "eightfold path tests" online are thin quizzes with unsourced claims.

**Promise.** A private self-check built on the discourses' own definitions, with concrete practices for the factors that need attention, each tied to a quoted passage you can read in full.

**Wedge.** Search ("eightfold path test", "right speech meaning", "right livelihood examples") lands on the check or on a chapter; results are shareable wheels.

**Why now.** High-quality public-domain translations (SuttaCentral, CC0) make a fully sourced, free guide possible.

**Activation metric.** A completed check (`check_done`). Share loop: `result_open`.

**First revenue event.** Donations (`SUPPORT_URL`), or a paid ebook of the chapters on other hosting.

**v1 deliberately won't:** claim to measure spiritual progress; "fix" or rank traditions; mention Maitreya; give medical or psychological advice; store answers anywhere but the visitor's browser.
