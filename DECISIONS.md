# Decisions

1. **App first, book second, both in v1.** The open question "book or app first?" is settled by shipping the self-check (the note's EightFoldHelper) with a short book of nine chapters, one per factor plus an introduction, that also serve as the search pages.
2. **No "fix Right View" framing.** The note's premise ("this book is here to fix Right View") is exactly what the brief flags as offensive to practitioners. The site describes the path as the discourses do, and frames itself as a reflection aid. Maitreya is cut, per the brief.
3. **The discourses speak; the site stays modest.** Every definition is quoted from SN 45.8; chapters quote further passages; the site's own commentary is short, plain and labelled as its own. The generated-looking explainer from the notes isn't reused.
4. **Bhikkhu Sujato's CC0 translations, verified by machine.** The brief asks for public-domain translations. SuttaCentral publishes Sujato's as CC0; the pipeline takes passages by segment from the published data and checks the text and the license every week.
5. **Statements mirror the definitions.** Each self-check statement corresponds to a part of the factor's canonical definition (for example right speech's four abstentions), so the check reflects the teaching rather than the site's opinions.
6. **Lay wording for right action.** Sujato's SN 45.8 says "unchastity" (the monastic standard); the self-check uses the lay precept (sexual misconduct), and the chapter explains the difference.
7. **Name: Eightfold Helper.** Jack's own name for the tool, and what people search for. The domain (effectivebuddhism.com) is left to the domain rollout session because something may already be live there.
